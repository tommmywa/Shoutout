# Shoutout — Product Brief

## Product
A mobile platform where fans purchase artist shoutouts in songs, while each shoutout contributes to the recognition history of a specific person (Name Identity).

## Phase 1 goal
Deliver a complete studio-shoutout product:
- Fan discovery
- Artist/song browsing
- Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum shoutouts
- Name Identity selection/creation
- Checkout/payment
- Shoutout order tracking
- Artist song and shoutout management
- Artist recording/publishing
- Social Capital points
- Name profiles
- Leaderboard
- Essential moderation/identity administration

Phase 1 uses internal, non-transferable points. Do not build real token trading.

## Phase 2 direction
Add live streaming and real-time shoutouts without replacing the Phase 1 model. Live shoutouts should become another source of recognition events.

## Core mental model
User → Name Identity → Shoutout → Song/Artist → Recognition Event → Social Capital

## Product promise
Make recognition feel tangible: fans can pay to get a name heard, see the resulting moment, and watch that recognition accumulate over time.

## Non-goals for Phase 1
- Live streaming
- Live music sessions
- Tradable crypto/token economy
- Complex trading charts
- Automated identity merging
- Full creator social network
