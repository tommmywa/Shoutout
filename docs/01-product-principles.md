# Product Principles

1. **Recognition first.** The emotional product is being heard and remembered, not trading.
2. **Identity before name string.** A display name is not a person.
3. **Buyer and recipient are different roles.** A fan can purchase a shoutout for someone else.
4. **Every meaningful action has a state.** Do not hide pending, failed, rejected, or published states.
5. **Studio first, live-ready.** Phase 1 must be complete without live features, but the data and navigation should accommodate them later.
6. **Points before token.** Social Capital is represented as internal points in Phase 1.
7. **Events are extensible.** Studio and future live events should feed the same recognition system.
8. **Trust matters.** Engagement should not be treated as equally trustworthy by default.
9. **Consent matters.** A named person's identity/consent is distinct from the purchaser.
10. **Mobile-first.** Primary interactions should work comfortably with one hand and small screens.
11. **Progressive disclosure.** Keep complex mechanics behind understandable user-facing language.
12. **No invented product behavior.** If a requirement is missing, flag it rather than silently creating business rules.
